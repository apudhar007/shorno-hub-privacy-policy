Shorno Hub ("Shorno Hub", "we", "our", or "us") is a business management application designed for jewelry shops and related businesses. This Privacy Policy explains how Shorno Hub accesses, collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use our application.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, Shorno Hub may handle the following types of information.

1.1 Account Information

If you use the optional Google Sign-In feature for backup or account-related services, we may process information associated with your Google account, such as:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Account identifier
  • Authentication information provided through Google Sign-In

Google Sign-In is optional. Shorno Hub can be used without creating a Shorno Hub account when account-based backup functionality is not required.

1.2 Business and Shop Information

Shorno Hub allows users to enter and manage their own business records. This may include:

  • Shop or business information
  • Customer names and contact information
  • Supplier names and contact information
  • Employee information
  • Customer balances and due amounts
  • Supplier balances and payment information
  • Sales and purchase records
  • Stock and inventory information
  • Jewelry specifications, weights, karat/purity, rates, and prices
  • Mortgage and pawn records
  • Interest and repayment records
  • Income and expense records
  • Employee salary, advance, bonus, deduction, and payment records
  • Cash book and financial ledger records
  • Product photographs
  • Invoice and receipt information

This information is entered by the user and is used to provide the business management features of the application.

1.3 Photos and Camera

Shorno Hub may request access to your device camera when you choose to photograph jewelry or other products.

Camera access is used to:

  • Capture product photographs
  • Attach photographs to inventory or sales records
  • Support product identification and record management

Shorno Hub does not use the camera for continuous background monitoring.

1.4 Device and Technical Information

Depending on the services and features you use, third-party services integrated into Shorno Hub may process certain technical information, such as:

  • App version
  • Device information
  • Operating system information
  • Crash information
  • Performance information
  • Diagnostic information
  • Anonymous or aggregated usage information

2. How We Use Information

Information handled by Shorno Hub may be used to:

  • Provide and operate the application's business management features
  • Authenticate users
  • Manage customer and supplier records
  • Manage inventory and stock
  • Process sales and purchase records
  • Calculate gold and silver weights, rates, and related amounts
  • Manage mortgages and interest calculations
  • Manage expenses and employee salaries
  • Maintain cash books and financial records
  • Generate invoices and receipts
  • Provide notifications and reminders
  • Provide backup and restore functionality
  • Detect crashes and technical problems
  • Monitor application performance
  • Improve application reliability and functionality
  • Display advertisements where advertising features are enabled
  • Protect the application and its users from unauthorized access or misuse

We do not use your business records for purposes unrelated to providing or supporting Shorno Hub's functionality unless otherwise disclosed or required by law.

3. Local Storage

Shorno Hub is designed to support offline business management.

Business records may be stored locally on your Android device using a local database. This can include customer information, supplier information, stock, sales, mortgage records, expenses, salaries, and other business records entered into the application.

Local data remains on your device unless you delete the data, clear the application's data, uninstall the application, or use a backup or synchronization feature that transfers the relevant data.

You are responsible for maintaining appropriate backups of important business records.

4. Google Drive Backup

Shorno Hub may provide an optional backup and restore feature using Google Drive.

When you choose to create a backup, the application may transfer your Shorno Hub database or backup file to your Google Drive account.

Google Drive backups are initiated by the user and are associated with the Google account selected by the user.

We do not access your Google Drive files unrelated to Shorno Hub's backup functionality.

You are responsible for maintaining access to the Google account used for backup and for protecting your Google account credentials.

5. Security and App Lock

Shorno Hub provides security features designed to help prevent unauthorized access to information stored on your device.

  • Four-digit PIN protection
  • Biometric authentication
  • Android's supported biometric authentication mechanisms

Biometric authentication is handled through Android's supported biometric security framework. Shorno Hub does not receive or store your fingerprint or other biometric information.

The effectiveness of these security features also depends on the security of your Android device and operating system.

6. Notifications

If you grant notification permission, Shorno Hub may send notifications relating to application functionality, such as:

  • Mortgage or repayment reminders
  • Supplier payment reminders
  • Low-stock notifications
  • Other business-related reminders

Notifications can be controlled through your Android device settings where supported.

7. PDF Invoices, Receipts and Files

Shorno Hub may allow you to generate invoices, receipts, reports, or other documents.

Files generated by the application may be stored on your device or shared by you using Android's available sharing functionality.

You control where and with whom you share these files.

8. Bluetooth and Thermal Printers

Shorno Hub may optionally support Bluetooth-connected thermal printers.

If you choose to use this functionality, the application may use the relevant Bluetooth permissions required by Android to connect to and communicate with a compatible printer.

Bluetooth access is used for printing business documents such as receipts or invoices.

13. Account and Associated Data Deletion

Shorno Hub values your privacy. If you have used the optional Google Sign-In feature for backup and account-related services, you may request deletion of your account and associated online data at any time.

How to Request Account & Data Deletion

  1. From within the app: Open Shorno Hub → Drawer / Settings → Account Settings → Delete Account.
  2. Via email: Send an email to psdhar007@gmail.com with the subject "Shorno Hub - Account Deletion Request". Include your registered email address or Google account details so we can identify the account.

Data That Will Be Permanently Deleted

  • Your user profile information, including name, email address, and account UID
  • Online backup metadata and synchronization tokens
  • User coins and activity preferences stored on our cloud servers

Data Kept on Your Device

Local shop records stored on your Android device remain under your local control unless you clear the application's storage, delete the records through the app where applicable, or uninstall the app.

Google Drive Backups

Backup files stored in your personal Google Drive may remain there unless you delete them from Google Drive. Account deletion from Shorno Hub does not automatically remove copies that you have stored in your personal Google Drive.

Data Retention Period

Account deletion requests sent by email are processed within 7 business days. Once processed, applicable personal data is permanently removed from our active servers, subject to any data we are legally required or legitimately permitted to retain.
